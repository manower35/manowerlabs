Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the mobile QR scanner work without an App Store download?

The scanner runs directly in any modern mobile browser (Chrome, Safari) using secure camera permissions (`MediaDevices API`). Staff members simply connect to your local PC or secure tunnel URL—there is no need to download or update apps from Google Play or Apple App Store.

Is my business and customer data safe from competitors?

Yes, 100%. All databases and records reside strictly on your own physical computer or local network. We do not store your customer records, financial numbers, or order books on public multi-tenant clouds.

What happens if our computer crashes or we get a new PC?

Every system we build includes 1-click backup scripts (`backup.bat`) and portable database files (SQLite). Copying your backup folder or pen drive to any new computer restores the entire system in under two minutes.

Can these tools connect with our existing Excel sheets?

Yes. Seamless Excel importing and exporting is a core design feature of all our desktop utilities and web dashboards.
